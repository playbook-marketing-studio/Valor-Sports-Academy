The space

A real training floor, not a rented gym corner

Valor is a working sports academy. The same turf and weight room our athletes train on every day is open to outside groups by the hour, so your team gets a floor built for practice, not a room with the tables pushed aside.

Who rents it

Teams, groups and coaches across the Tri-Cities

When the field is frozen, the gym is booked, or the group has outgrown a garage, this is where they come.

Youth teams

Youth teams book the turf for practices and off-season work when outdoor fields are dark, wet or frozen.

Fitness groups

CrossFit and other adult fitness groups run their own sessions on the turf and in the weight room.

Basketball programs

Youth basketball programs use the space for conditioning and strength work between games.

Questions

Renting the space, answered

How much does it cost?

$125 per hour for the turf floor and the weight room. Tell us the day, time and how many hours you need and we confirm what is open.

Who rents the space?

Youth teams looking for indoor practice space, CrossFit and fitness groups, and youth basketball programs have all trained here. Coaches and trainers who need a floor for their own athletes are welcome too.

Where is the facility?

1973 Fowler Street in Richland, next to the Chef'Store and Cost Less Carpet. Groups come from Kennewick, Pasco, West Richland and across the Tri-Cities.

How do I book?

Send the request form above or call or text 509-987-4612 with the day and time you want. A coach confirms availability with you directly.

Need a floor this week?

Send the day and time. A coach gets back to you to confirm.
