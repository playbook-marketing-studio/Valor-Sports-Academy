**Getting in**
1. On your phone, go to app.valorsportsacademywa.com/register
2. Sign up with coreybibe30@gmail.com (Corey) or mbibe@eou.edu (Michael) and pick a password. It puts you on the staff side automatically.
3. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, so it opens like an app.

A quick tour pops up the first time you log in. You can reopen it under More > How the app works. Then open Athletes and tap **Test Omar** at the top. That's my test kid with everything filled in, so you can see what a full profile looks like. Tap **View as athlete** on his page to see exactly what a parent sees.

**How to use it**

*New leads*
1. Anyone who filled out the website form shows under **Waiting on a text** on the Assessments tab. Tap **Text**. The message is written for you, and they move to **Reached out**.
2. When they reply with a time, tap **Book a time**. If they pass, tap **Not interested**.

*Saturday assessments*
1. When a kid shows up, tap **Check in** next to their name. Their page opens.
2. Enter their test results and the one or two things to work on first.
3. Tap **Show login QR** and have the parent scan it to set up their login.
4. Take payment with **Paid cash** or **Paid Venmo**.
5. Tap **Put in a class**. The **Next steps** list at the top of their page shows what's left.
6. Walk-in? Tap the red **+** at the bottom.

*During the week*
1. **Today** shows that day's classes and who still needs a text.
2. **Class log** (under More): pick the class and type the weight each kid used.
3. Tap **Log a class visit** on a kid's page each time they train, so their pack counts down.
4. To change a kid's days or week: their page, **Training** tab, **Change days or week**.
