If a holiday results in a cancelled class and that class has already received 4 classes that month, no refunds/makeups will be given.

## 2. Express Assumption of Risks.

I understand and agree that the activities at Valor are inherently dangerous. Those activities involve but are not limited to, lifting, running, jumping, sport-specific movements and skills. These activities present significant risk of serious disfigurement, injury and death, including but not limited to life-changing permanent injuries such as partial or total paralysis, paraplegia, quadriplegia, neck and/or back/spinal fractures and/or impairment, brain injuries, vegetative state, and brain death. These risks include the risk of inadequate or negligent supervision of Participants in their activities. Valor intentionally keeps its Staff/Participant ratios reasonable in order to minimize these risks, but it is impossible for Staff to watch all Participants at all times, and it is impossible for Staff to always be in sufficient physical proximity to prevent all injuries to all Participants. Some Participants and their parents lower these risks by scheduling any class or event in which there is one instructor for each student. Even with our classes and events, however, it is impossible for staff to eliminate all risks. Even with classes and events, however, staff may be momentarily distracted by other events in the gym, or may simply be unable to see a fall or other dangerous event starting to occur. Even with our classes and events, staff may simply not be in a physical position where they can prevent an injury given how quickly some injuries occur.

Severe injuries are relatively rare but cannot always be avoided even in the exercise of reasonable or even extreme care. Further, I acknowledge that statistics are periodically reported showing that athletic training may have more injuries than many other sports including “contact sports” such as football, soccer, hockey and basketball.

I fully understand that these risks may be caused by my own actions or inactions, the actions or inactions of others participating in the event, the conditions in which the activity or event takes place, or the negligence of the “releasees” named above; and that there may be other risks either not known to me or not readily foreseeable at this time; and I fully accept and assume all such risks and all responsibility for injury, harm, losses, cost, and damages I may incur as a result of my participation in the activities.
